## Full description

Closer Cards is a conversation game for two people on a date, a long drive or a quiet night in. Share one phone, flip a card, and take turns answering.

HOW IT WORKS
• 36 questions in three sets of 12
• Set 1 is light: small pleasures, memories, favourites
• Set 2 moves to values and experiences
• Set 3 goes deeper, and either of you may pass on any question
• A banner shows who answers first, and it alternates every card
• Tap a card to reveal the question, then Next or Skip
• Optional timer for each question: Next fills in as a gentle pacing cue, and never locks

MADE FOR THE TWO OF YOU, NOT YOUR PHONE
• Fully offline: the app has no internet permission
• No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Nothing you say or type is stored or sent anywhere
• Light and dark themes, and it respects your system font size

Player names are optional, and you can end the session after any set.

Closer Cards is inspired by research on how structured, mutual self-disclosure builds closeness (Aron et al., 1997). All 36 questions in the app are original and are not the questions used in that study.
