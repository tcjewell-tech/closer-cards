# Closer Cards privacy policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Closer Cards is an offline card game for two people sharing one phone. This policy covers the Closer Cards Android app.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Closer Cards does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data.

- The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your phone.
- There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no third-party services.
- Player names are optional. If you type them, they are held in memory for the current session only and are gone when the app closes.
- The app does not keep a record of which questions you saw or skipped.
- The app requests no device permissions and takes no part in device backup.

## Children

Closer Cards is written for adults and is not directed at children. Because it collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children.

## Changes

If a later version changes any of the above, this policy will be updated and the effective date changed before that version is released.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: use the developer contact email shown on the Closer Cards page on Google Play.
